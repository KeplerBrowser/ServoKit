Navigation policy

Hosts can use these links to check that navigation-decision callbacks receive ordinary allowed requests and deliberately denied requests.

Policy targets

Expected policy for this fixture set: allow same-origin HTTP(S) smoke links, deny URLs containing blocked-by-policy, and deny unsupported custom schemes.
